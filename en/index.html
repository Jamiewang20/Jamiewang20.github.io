Jianming Wang王建明

I am an investor drawn to frontier technology. I like to go deep into the fields I follow: tracing an idea back to its origins, understanding how a system works, and listening to the people trying to build what comes next.

My investments span enterprise services, AI, hardware, and robotics. In recent years, my main focus has been AI-powered robotics and physical AI—how intelligence learns to perceive, understand, and act in the physical world.

I hold a master’s degree in Finance from the Wang Yanan Institute for Studies in Economics at Xiamen University, and a bachelor’s degree in Aircraft Propulsion Engineering from Xiamen University. I previously worked as an auditor at KPMG and am a CFA charterholder.

Writing and conversation are part of how I do research. I run Smart Note, a blog on embodied AI, where I interview researchers and founders and explore human data, world models, and the history of robotics. Through the ReSpark podcast, I continue these conversations in a format that leaves room for questions, uncertainty, and the thinking behind each idea.
